# EvidenceArc name availability review

**Checked:** October 29, 2026  
**Purpose:** Preliminary public-record and marketplace screening before launch. This is not legal advice or a legal clearance opinion.

## Summary

**Do not treat EvidenceArc as cleared yet.** The U.S. and UK exact-wording trademark searches below returned no matches, and searches did not surface an exact career-coaching app-store listing. However:

- `evidencearc.com` is registered and currently displays a Squarespace “Coming Soon” page.
- The exact `@evidencearc` YouTube handle resolves to a channel titled **Evidence Arc**. The channel currently has no content.
- The owner of the domain and channel could not be identified from these checks. They may belong to the EvidenceArc project owner; confirm that before considering a rename.

If the project owner controls both, the findings do not by themselves require a new brand. If either belongs to an unrelated party and cannot be acquired or licensed, the combination of an unavailable exact-match `.com` and an existing exact-name YouTube channel is a meaningful launch and discoverability risk. In that case, consider a different name before investing further.

The product defaults to English and also offers Spanish, French, and German. No target launch countries were stated, so this pass focused on the U.S. and UK public trademark registries and English-language app/social searches. It does not establish availability in the EU, Canada, or other markets.

## Recommendation

First confirm whether the project owner controls `evidencearc.com` and the `@evidencearc` YouTube channel. If both are controlled by the owner, keeping EvidenceArc is reasonable pending a broader trademark search for the actual launch countries. If they are third-party assets and cannot be acquired, change the name before launch rather than relying on a different domain and an occupied exact-name handle.

One **working replacement candidate** is **PractiStory**. A preliminary public search did not surface an exact career-preparation brand; the U.S. and UK exact-wording searches returned 0 trademark records, and `practistory.com` returned no RDAP record on the check date. This is only a brainstorming candidate, not a cleared or guaranteed-available name. Search all relevant registries, app listings, domains, and social handles before adopting it.

## Limits

Search results can be incomplete or delayed, handles and domains can change at any time, and an exact-wording search is not a full trademark clearance. No legal advice is provided. A trademark professional should review exact and confusingly similar marks, related goods/services, unregistered use, and the relevant countries before launch or filing.
